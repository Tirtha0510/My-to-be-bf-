things I couldn't say properly

A letter for you.

Ek saal pehla aapde ek bija ne cross thaya hata, but tyare tu tara life ma alag hato and hu mara life ma. Honestly, kone vicharyu hase ke exact ek year pachi aapde ek bija sathe aa rite date karishu? Etle mane aa badhu just coincidence nathi lagtu. It feels like life quietly brought us to the same place at the right time.

Hu bau j blessed feel karu chhu ke tu mne madyo. Mane khabar che har ek divas perfect nahi hoy, and honestly perfect banavani jarur pan nathi. Mane bas evu joie che ke sara divaso ma aapde hasi ne enjoy kariye, ane difficult divaso ma ek bija thi door na thaiye. Aapde sathe sathe grow kariye, samjiye, ane aa bond ne apdi rite strong banaviye.

Tari ek vaat je mara heart ma bau deep gayi hati e hati jyare tu mne surprise karva Vadodara aavyo hato, just because hu low feel karti hati. Tyaare mane genuinely feel thayu ke haan, Bhagwan life ma sara loko pan mokle che. And for me, tu e j person che. Taro effort, taru care, tari softness — aa badhu hu notice karu chhu, even when hu bolti nathi.

Tu perfect nathi, and hu pan nathi. But mara mate tu more than perfect che, because tu mane je rite feel karave che e koi perfection karta vadhu important che. Taro smile, tari eyes, tari silliness, tari passion, ane sometimes tari evi harkato ke hu bas “aa maru cutuu che” kahi ne smile kari dau. 😭💙

Ane haan, hu tane badha karta alag rite like karu chhu. Etlu ke sometimes “I like you” kehvu pan nani line lage che. But hu jaldi ma kai label nathi aapva mangti. I just want to enjoy what we have, keep being honest with you, and let us become whatever we are meant to become.

So, my Chirkutyaa Cutuuudaaa…
I likeeee youuu so, so, so freaking muchhh. 🤤😘
